We're here to help. Find quick answers below or reach out to our team.

How do I create a video?

  • Sign in with your Google account.
  • Choose a template or type your own AI prompt.
  • Upload your photos (if the template needs them).
  • Tap Create and wait for your video to be generated.

How do credits work?

  • Each video generation uses credits from your account.
  • You get free credits when you sign up.
  • You can buy more credits at any time from the Plans screen.

Purchases & Refunds

  • All purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store).
  • For refunds, contact Apple or Google support or email us for assistance.
